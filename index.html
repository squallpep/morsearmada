Frequently asked questions

How do I fire? Tap a cell on the enemy grid to see its coordinate, then key the row letter and the column letter on the telegraph key. Short press = dot, long press = dash. Wait a moment (or press “Send”) to confirm each letter.

What is free? Academy lessons 1–5, the first 3 Arena automatons, Chapter 1 of the story, the Captains’ Duel and the Codex.

How do I unlock the full game? Tap any content with the gold “Full game” badge, or “Unlock the whole game” on the Home screen. It is a single one-time purchase: no subscription, no ads.

I bought the game on another device / reinstalled it. Open the Workshop (settings) and tap “Restore purchases”, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.

I can’t hear the Morse tones. Check the volume and that Sound is on in the Workshop. Vibrations can also be turned on there.

Privacy: see our privacy policy. In short: no account, no ads, no data collected.
