Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Morse Armada is a game for learning Morse code. We collect as little data as possible — in fact, none.

What the app stores

  • Your progress (lessons, stars, settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent to a server.
  • No account is required. We never ask for your name, e-mail address or phone number.
  • No advertising, no ad tracking, no analytics, no sale of data.
  • The game does not use the microphone, camera, location or contacts.

In-app purchase

“Morse Armada Complete” is processed entirely by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play. We never see your payment details. The purchase is verified directly on your device using Apple’s or Google’s signature; no third-party service is involved and no purchase data is sent to us.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from children.

Deleting your data

In the app: Workshop › Reset progress. Deleting the app removes all data stored on your device.
